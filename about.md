# Thorny

A little spike companion that lives in the Geometry Dash main menu.

Thorny hovers around the menu, flies between random points, and shows a small
speech bubble with things to say. He watches what you do and changes his mood
and his lines accordingly:

- **Started a level** - wishes you luck.
- **Died** - a sympathetic comment.
- **Beat the level** - celebrates with you.
- **Came back from the editor** - asks what you built.
- **Doing nothing for a while** - eventually dozes off and talks to you.

He only appears in the main menu. He reacts in **English or Russian** (with
automatic language detection), and you can toggle him, his movement, his
dialogue, the frequency, the size, and the language from the mod's settings.
